Use

Tadhkiruk is an aid for worship and time management. By using it you agree to these terms.

Religious content

Athkar and duas are drawn from Hisn al-Muslim, the Quran from the standard Hafs text, and hadiths from the recognised books of Sunnah with the source shown. We aim for accuracy, but this content is not a substitute for asking scholars about rulings. If you find a mistake, write to us and we will correct it.

Prayer times and Qibla

Times and direction are calculated from your location and the method you choose, and may differ by a few minutes from your local mosque's timetable. Check with the official authority in your country, and do not rely on the alert alone for critical matters, since device power-saving settings can affect delivery.

Zakah calculator

The calculator is an estimate based on the figures you enter. For special cases, consult a fatwa authority.

Optional subscription (Tadhkiruk Plus)

  • Worship (Quran, athkar, prayer times, Qibla) and the zakah calculator are always free and never locked behind the subscription.
  • Money and Family are free within limits: 5 open debts, 1 savings goal, 20 people in the family tree, and 3 documents. Daily life (groceries, home notes) is free.
  • Tadhkiruk Plus lifts these limits and adds the finance report, expenses over all time, PDF export, family-file export and import, and the image card and text editor for occasion messages (condolence and newborn messages stay free).
  • Nothing you saved is ever locked: if the subscription ends, your data stays as it is and you can still view and edit it; the limits apply only to adding new items.
  • There is no free trial. Plans: monthly or yearly at the regular price; the final price is shown in your currency in the App Store or Google Play before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple or Google account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; renewal is charged within the 24 hours before it ends.
  • Cancel on iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Tadhkiruk. On Android, in Google Play: Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Tadhkiruk, Cancel. The subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.
  • Payment and refunds are governed by App Store and Google Play policies; we do not handle your card data.
  • On Apple devices, Apple's standard end user license agreement also applies.
