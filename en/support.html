Support

Email support@tadhkiruk.app. Please include your device, Android version, and what happened.

I did not get the Adhan alert

  • Open the app, go to Worship, then Prayer notifications, and tap the one-minute Adhan test.
  • Allow notifications and exact alarms, and exclude the app from battery optimisation.
  • On Samsung phones: Settings, Battery, Background usage limits, and add Tadhkiruk to Never sleeping apps.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Google Play: Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Tadhkiruk, Cancel subscription.

Restoring my subscription on a new phone

Open the subscription screen and tap Restore purchases, using the same Google account you subscribed with.

I found a mistake in religious text

Send us the text, where it appears, and the source you are checking against, and we will correct it in the next update.

Delete my data

See Delete data.
