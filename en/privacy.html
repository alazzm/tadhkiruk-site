Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Tadhkiruk (package: com.tadhkiruk.app) is built to respect your privacy. This page explains what the app does and does not collect.

Data we use

The app uses your location only while in use, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Notes, reminders, progress, lists, and financial entries are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

Notifications

Notification and exact-alarm permissions are used for prayer alerts and reminders you choose to enable. You can turn them off in app or system settings.

Backups

Local backups are encrypted with a password you choose. There is no cloud sync and nothing is uploaded automatically.

Audio and external content

Audio you request (Quran recitations and Hisn al-Muslim athkar) may be streamed or downloaded from third-party servers identified in the app. Like any website, those servers see your connection address (IP) when you play. Optional downloads never start without your action.

Subscription and purchases

The optional subscription (Tadhkiruk Plus) is handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never receive your card or payment details. Your subscription status and free-trial dates are kept locally on your device. Quran, athkar, prayer times, and Qibla are always free.

Ads and sharing

The app does not rely on ads, does not sell your personal data, and uses no advertising trackers. Sharing to WhatsApp or other apps happens only when you choose it, through your device's share sheet.

Children

The app collects no personal data from any user, including children, and shows no ads to anyone.

Deletion and control

You can delete data from its section or uninstall the app to remove all local data. See Delete data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.
