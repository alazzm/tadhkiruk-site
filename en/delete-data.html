Delete data

Tadhkiruk creates no account and uploads none of your data to a server. Everything stays on your device and you are in control of it.

Delete some data

Inside the app you can delete notes, reminders, financial entries, and lists from their own sections.

Delete all data

  • Android: Settings, Apps, Tadhkiruk, Storage, Clear data.
  • Or uninstall the app and all local data is erased.

Subscription

Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play: Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.

Backups

Any backup file you created stays where you saved it, so delete it yourself if you no longer need it.
